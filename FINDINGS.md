# Findings: Land Clearing, Forestry Mulching and Stump Removal Cost Register (US, 2026)

All figures are as published; no figures were computed except ratios noted as such. Version 2026-10-21.

- **Aggregator vs contractor forestry mulching gap.** HomeGuide lists $400–$800/acre and HomeAdvisor $400–$1,500/acre (avg $500). Contractor-published per-acre prices start higher: Pierce Land Clearing (TX) $1,300–$4,000 by stem size; Cut Brush (TN) $1,500–$4,000 for mulching edges; Byrd Land Co. (OR, market figures) $1,500–$3,500 average.
- **Hourly mulching rates converge** at roughly $125–$300: HomeGuide $125–$300, Byrd $150–$300, Big Frog (GA/TN/NC) $150–$250. Heavy/excavator-mounted work runs to $400/hr (HomeGuide, HomeAdvisor, Byrd).
- **Machine-day pricing:** Pierce lists $4,000/day high-horsepower mulcher (plus transport) and $3,000/day skid steer mulcher (transport included).
- **Land clearing per acre:** HomeGuide $1,200–$8,000 (light $1,200–$2,500; heavy $4,500–$8,000); Angi $500–$5,600, national average project $3,778 (2,416 projects).
- **Stump grinding:** HomeGuide $2–$6/inch with $80–$160 minimums. Contractors: Simply Stump Grinding (IN) $3–$5/inch, $185 minimum; Steve's Stump Grinding (UT) $7.50/inch, $250 minimum ($10/inch with cleanup, $350 minimum). The published contractor minimums exceed the aggregator minimum range.
